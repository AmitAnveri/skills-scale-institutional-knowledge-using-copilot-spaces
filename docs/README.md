# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

OctoAcme manages cross-functional work through a customer-first, iterative lifecycle. Teams initiate and validate an idea, plan scope and backlog, execute and track delivery, release and verify changes, then capture learnings through retrospectives and continuous improvement. The Project Manager coordinates delivery, risks, and communications; the Product Manager owns outcomes and prioritization; developers, QA, and stakeholders collaborate to deliver and validate the work.

- [Project Initiation Guide](octoacme-project-initiation.md) — validate the need, align stakeholders, and decide whether to proceed to planning.
- [Project Planning](octoacme-project-planning.md) — turn approved work into a prioritized, estimated backlog and delivery plan.
- [Execution & Tracking](octoacme-execution-and-tracking.md) — coordinate day-to-day delivery, quality checks, progress reporting, and blocker escalation.
- [Risk Management & Communication](octoacme-risks-and-communication.md) — identify, assess, mitigate, and communicate risks and dependencies.
- [Release & Deployment Guide](octoacme-release-and-deployment.md) — prepare, deploy, verify, and, when needed, roll back releases.
- [Retrospective & Continuous Improvement](octoacme-retrospective-and-continuous-improvement.md) — capture learnings and track actionable improvements.
- [Roles & Personas](octoacme-roles-and-personas.md) — responsibilities, goals, and communication patterns for common project roles.
